Designed to stay out of the way

Less software interaction is the feature.

CoastOps should earn its place in the job by reducing interruptions, not by giving you more screens to maintain.

01

Freeform photos

No front / rear / interior category tap before every shot. Capture first. Organize around the job.

02

Minimal typing

Confirm what matters. Avoid rebuilding information the job already makes obvious.

03

Surface, don’t nag

Existing conditions, extras, and missing finish proof appear when they are useful — not as constant checklists.

04

You keep control

Prices, condition records, add-ons, and customer proof stay reviewable before they go out.
